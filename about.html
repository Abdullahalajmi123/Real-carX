Working Hours:

CarX is a simple and efficient CRM system designed specifically for car dealerships. Our goal is to make communication between customers and dealerships faster, easier, and more organized.

What We Do:

We help customers submit inquiries, book maintenance appointments, schedule test drives, and place vehicle purchase orders all in one place. At the same time, dealership staff can easily update and manage customer information and the cars they own.

Why CarX?

CarX improves customer service by keeping everything clear, accessible, and organized. Our system reduces paperwork, saves time, and helps dealerships track all interactions with customers smoothly.
